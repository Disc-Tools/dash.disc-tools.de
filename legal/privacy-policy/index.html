Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Email: info@disc-tools.de

2. Hosting, CDN & Security

The Disc-Tools service - including the dashboard at dash.disc-tools.de and the website at disc-tools.de - is operated on server infrastructure hosted by Hetzner in Helsinki, Finland. Content delivery and DDoS protection are provided through Cloudflare. All connections are encrypted via TLS.

3. Server Logs

Our web server and API log each request including your IP address, timestamp, HTTP method, requested path, HTTP status code, user agent, and any error messages. These logs are automatically rotated and kept for a maximum of 30 days. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - our legitimate interest in detecting and defending against abuse.

4. Cookies and local storage

Authentication cookies are required to provide the dashboard. Your optional analytics and advertising choices are saved in your browser’s local storage under disc-tools-dashboard-consent-v4. Umami and Google AdSense are loaded only after you consent to their respective categories.

  • dash_token - JWT session token for authentication (7 days)
  • oauth_state - CSRF protection token during login (10 minutes)

When you allow advertising, Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. See Google’s information about cookies.

5. Analytics

We use a self-hosted instance of Umami Analytics on our own domain (umami.disc-tools.de) for website analytics. The analytics script is loaded only after you consent to analytics in the cookie banner. Umami is configured not to use cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

6. Discord OAuth2 Login

When you log in via Discord OAuth2, we receive your Discord user ID, username, avatar, email address, and guild memberships from the Discord API. We use this data exclusively to authenticate you and provide the dashboard. Your IP address is SHA-256 hashed with a per-service salt before storage - the raw IP is never persisted. We store this hashed IP for session tracking and abuse prevention. Retention: Session records are deleted when you log out or revoke the session in the dashboard; the login token itself expires automatically after 7 days. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR - performance of a contract (providing the authenticated dashboard service).

7. VPN & Proxy Detection

For non-authenticated dashboard visitors, we query proxycheck.io with your IP address to detect VPN, proxy, or hosting connections. Your IP is transmitted to proxycheck.io for this purpose. If a VPN/proxy is detected, access is restricted unless you log in. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - our legitimate interest in protecting the service from automated abuse and scraping.

8. Username History

If you are in a Discord server where Disc-Tools is active, the bot may record changes to your username (old and new username, timestamp, and associated server ID). This feature can be disabled by the server owner. You can opt out of having your history publicly accessible via Profile Settings → Username History, which prevents anyone from looking up your past usernames. Stored usernames are retained until you request deletion.

9. Verification & Alt Detection

When you verify a Discord account through the bot, we store your Discord user ID, a SHA-256 hash of your IP address, and a verification timestamp. This data is used to detect alternate accounts (Premium feature). Retention: Verification records are retained for abuse detection and are deleted upon your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the verification service, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for abuse detection.

10. Premium Subscriptions & Stripe

Premium payments are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe, we transmit your Discord user ID to Stripe. Stripe returns a customer ID and subscription ID, which we store. The following data is stored in our database:

  • Discord user ID
  • Stripe customer and subscription IDs
  • Subscription status (active/inactive)
  • Start and expiry dates
  • Transaction history (type, duration, gift metadata)

Retention: Payment records are retained for the duration of the subscription plus 3 years after cancellation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with § 147 AO / § 257 HGB - statutory retention obligations under German tax and commercial law). In specific cases, data may be retained for up to 10 years for tax purposes. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to data they process independently.

11. GIF Uploads

When you upload a GIF via the bot, we store your Discord user ID, the GIF file on our server infrastructure, the original filename, a display name, optional tags, an NSFW flag, file dimensions, file size, view count, and moderation status. GIFs are publicly accessible by their assigned ID. Prohibited content (including CSAM, extreme violence, hate speech) is removed. We report CSAM to the relevant authorities. For age-restricted (NSFW) content, we require a date of birth for age verification. You may request deletion of your uploaded GIFs at any time by contacting us. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR - performance of a contract - and Art. 6(1)(c) for legal obligations regarding content moderation.

12. Data Export Requests

You can request a copy of your personal data at any time via Profile Settings → Data Request (Art. 15 GDPR). The download link is sent to your Discord account email address or to an email address you specify, and is valid for 7 days and can only be used once. The export is generated on demand and the download link is deleted after use or expiry. For abuse prevention, a new data request can only be made once every 30 days. Storage: the email address you specify for delivery is stored in your account settings until you change it or request deletion; the timestamp of your last request is stored to enforce the 30-day limit. Data export emails are sent through our email delivery provider (see Section 16). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 15 GDPR.

13. Discord Profile Widget

Disc-Tools offers an optional stats widget for Discord profiles (currently restricted to the operator's own accounts). Enabling the widget stores your Discord user ID so the bot can refresh your widget statistics; disabling the widget deletes this record. You can request deletion at any time. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR - performance of a contract.

14. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Automated checks (such as VPN/proxy detection or alternate-account detection) only support manual decisions and security measures; they do not make decisions with legal effect about you.

15. International Data Transfers

Some of the service providers listed in Section 16 are based in the United States (Discord, Stripe, Cloudflare, proxycheck.io, Resend). Where personal data is transferred to countries outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is based on appropriate safeguards pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR, in particular the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) which these providers offer for such transfers, or the provider's location in a country covered by an adequacy decision. Resend additionally processes our emails in our selected EU region (Ireland). Our hosting provider Hetzner is located within the EU.

16. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party except as necessary for service operation (see Sections 2, 3, 7, 10, 11, 12). Specifically:

  • Discord Inc. - OAuth2 authentication and bot API access
  • Stripe - payment processing
  • Cloudflare - CDN, DDoS protection, TLS termination
  • proxycheck.io - VPN/proxy detection (IP only, not linked to identity)
  • Resend - transactional email delivery for data export requests (recipient email address only, processed in our selected EU region, Ireland)
  • Hetzner - server hosting infrastructure

17. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR) - obtain confirmation and a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) - correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) - request deletion of your data
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR) - object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR) - where processing is based on consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, email info@disc-tools.de or contact us via the Support Server. For the right of access, you can also request a copy of your data directly in Profile Settings → Data Request without contacting us.

18. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced via our Support Server. The current version is always available on this page.
