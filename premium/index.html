Premium FAQ

Ask in the support server if you need help with an existing membership.

Can I buy Premium for myself or someone else?

Yes. Choose a monthly subscription, a non-renewing one-month purchase, or a one-month gift for another Discord user.

Where can I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open Manage subscription in your account. After cancellation, your access continues until the end of the paid billing period.

Does a one-time purchase renew?

A one-time purchase gives you one month of Premium. It ends after that month and does not renew automatically.
